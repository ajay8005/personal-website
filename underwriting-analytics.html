Methodology

Five results carry my recommendation. Each is a short piece of mathematics applied to raw data. The full derivations and prose are in the hand-written report on the Report tab.

1 · Break-even

BE = 1 − 0.325 − 0.085 = 0.59

Of each ceded premium dollar, 32.5 cents returns as ceding commission and 8.5 cents covers internal costs. Losses above 59 cents per dollar make the deal unprofitable.

2 · Experience base: pooled, not averaged

LR2018–22 = Σt LtΣt EPt = 0.478

Pooling weights each year by its earned exposure; a simple average would give the half-earned 2023 a full vote. The base keeps the bad 2021 year in: excluding your worst year requires a causal justification the submission does not provide.

3 · Trend drift

1.070.98 ≈ 1.092  ⇒  1.0924.5 ≈ 1.485,  1.0922.25 ≈ 1.22

Reconstruction costs inflate claims about 7% per year while the premium rate declines about 2% per year, so the loss ratio drifts about 9.2% per year. Trending runs from the experience midpoint to the treaty midpoint, about 4.5 years. I credit half, ×1.22, because the cost trend is a benchmark rather than program-specific.

4 · Catastrophe frequency bound

P(N6 = 0) = e−6μ ≥ 0.05  ⇒  μ ≤ ln 206 ≈ 0.5 yr−1

Six clean years is weak evidence of zero catastrophe risk: the observation is fully consistent with an event every two years. With 69% of insured value in CA, TX, and FL, I apply a benchmark load of 8 to 12 cents per premium dollar, 10 central.

5 · Expected loss ratio and result

ELR = 0.478 × 1.22 + 0.10 ≈ 0.68,  π = EP (1 − ELR − 0.325 − 0.085) < 0

Every defensible setting lands the band at 65 to 72%, above the 59% break-even: an expected loss of $30K to $65K per $500K ceded. My recommendation is therefore to restructure, through commission, sliding scale, loss corridor, or occurrence limit, with raw-data conditions attached.

Data integrity: the identity that failed

W = E + U  violated in 15 rows:  Σ(W − E − U) = −$26,100

Fifteen 2022 dwelling-fire rows carry phantom unearned premium on expired policies. Combined with uniform 13.6% claim-handling ratios, formulaic 17.65% reserves, 99% collections in all six years, and zero IBNR, the summary figures cannot be taken at face value. That is itself a finding about process, not just data.
